Karaoke

Lines late? Slide right to show them earlier.

Plays along with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube in your browser, or anything else your computer reports as now playing. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, free and community-made, so some songs have none. Only songs with timed lyrics work. Tamil, Hindi and other Indian scripts show romanised (ஒரு → ORU). While this skin is in use, the song's title and artist are sent to lrclib.net.

Cat

— Stroke it with your mouse or finger. Head and chin get purrs. The belly is a trap until it trusts you, and too much petting earns a swat.

—

Talk to it and it answers in a squeaky voice through this computer's speakers, and does a trick if you ask. Your browser turns your words into text (on this computer when it can, otherwise through Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari), and the text goes to an AI model on OpenRouter for its reply. Without a key it repeats what you say.

Tricks play right away, even over other skins

It has needs that drift through the day, even while this app is closed. When one runs low it asks: it waits by its bowl when hungry, asks to play when bored, wants petting when lonely, and naps when it's tired. Treats fill it up, games cheer it up and use up energy. It also reacts to your Mac: waves when Claude needs you, types along while Claude works, dances to music, naps when you're away 5 min (1 min after 11pm) and greets you when you're back. Otherwise: random mischief. With the Visualizer's microphone on, claps startle it.

Everything here takes over the panel immediately, no Apply. Stop it and your skin comes back.
